Data controls

Remove data from your device

You do not need a developer account to manage local data in Smart Home Dashboard.

Delete selected information

  • Saved hub: open Settings, find the hub and choose Remove. Its saved access token is removed from the app.
  • Recent activity: open Settings and choose Clear activity.
  • Entire local workspace: open Settings and choose Erase local data, then confirm. This removes saved workspace data, credentials and your explicit language preference.
  • Exported files: delete JSON or CSV copies from the file manager or the app you shared them with.
Erasure cannot be undone unless you kept an appropriate backup. Backups exclude tokens, so you must enter hub credentials again after restoration.

What these controls do not delete

Local erasure does not remove devices, automation rules, history or credentials stored by Home Assistant. To revoke a long-lived token at its source, use your Home Assistant account's security settings. Contact the hub operator for its records.

This release has no active developer-account registration or billing backend. Google and Samsung control their own store records. Contact Sakhi Apps through our GitHub support form if you need help with an applicable privacy request or deletion of support correspondence.

Do not send credentials or sensitive household information. For the complete explanation, read our privacy policy.
