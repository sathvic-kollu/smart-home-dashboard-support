Common questions

What do I need to connect?

Use a configured Home Assistant hub with a reachable HTTPS address and a valid long-lived access token. In Settings, enter a hub name, its HTTPS address and the token. Network access and a trusted HTTPS certificate are required.

Home Assistant authentication guide

Why won't a device respond?

Confirm the device works in Home Assistant and that the hub is reachable. Refresh the dashboard and check whether the hub exposes a supported control for that entity. Local inventory records are notes about devices and cannot control hardware.

Can this app pair Matter, Zigbee or branded devices?

Pairing and vendor authorization happen through your Home Assistant hub and its integrations. Settings can inspect which integrations are loaded and open official setup guides. A loaded integration does not guarantee that an individual device is paired or supported.

Can I use purchases or ad rewards?

Optional purchases and production advertising are disabled in Android version 1.0.0. Core hub controls and JSON backup remain available. There is no active subscription or developer-account registration in this version.

Where are the language and accessibility options?

Open Settings to choose one of eight languages, a light, dark or high-contrast theme, or reduced motion. Arabic uses right-to-left layout.

How do I remove data?

Use the local data controls in Settings. Read the deletion steps before removing a workspace or backup.
