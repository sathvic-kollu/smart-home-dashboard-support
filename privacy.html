Privacy policy

How your information is handled

Effective 5 October 2026 · Smart Home Dashboard, Android version 1.0.0

Smart Home Dashboard is provided by Sakhi Apps. It connects to a Home Assistant hub that you choose and configure. For privacy questions or requests, contact Sakhi Apps through our GitHub support form.

Current release: optional purchases, developer-account sign-in and production advertising are disabled. This policy describes that configuration. We will update this policy before activating services that change how information is processed.

Information kept on your device

The app saves hub names and addresses, device inventory, room assignments, notes, favourites, manual routines, display preferences, language choices and recent activity on your device. Activity is limited to the latest 100 entries. Hub readings and history are requested from your selected hub.

On Android, saved hub access tokens use Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. Tokens are excluded from local workspace records and JSON backups. A browser preview holds its hub token in memory and requires it again when that session ends.

Your hub connection

The app sends your access token, requests for entity states and history, and selected control commands directly to the HTTPS Home Assistant address you enter. Sakhi Apps does not receive your hub credentials, device inventory or commands on a developer-operated server in this release.

Your hub operator controls the hub's own logs, retention and access. Removing information from this app does not delete it from Home Assistant. Use your hub's settings or contact its operator for those requests.

Backups and exported files

JSON backups contain workspace settings and inventory, excluding credentials and activity. You choose where an exported or shared file goes. Exported files may include room names, device names and notes; protect them and delete any copies separately. This app disables Android's automatic app backup.

Consent software and advertising

The Android app includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Consent checks can contact Google even while ad serving is disabled. Google may process connection and device information associated with these checks under its privacy policy. Where applicable, the app presents privacy choices and a Settings entry for changing them.

Google Mobile Ads and a Unity mediation adapter are included for optional advertising, but production ad serving and Unity demand are not enabled in this release. When advertising is enabled in a later release, ad providers may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostics. See Google's SDK disclosure and Unity's privacy policy. Our app does not add hub tokens, room names, inventory or commands to ad requests.

No separate Firebase or application-analytics service is configured. The app does not sell your hub credentials or inventory.

Accounts and purchases

This release does not offer developer-account registration, sign-in or purchases. No production receipt-verification backend is configured. If these services are enabled later, we will identify their operator, data uses, retention and deletion process before collecting billing or account information. App-store account and transaction records are separately controlled by Google or Samsung.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you remove it using the app's Settings or uninstall the app, subject to platform behaviour. Removing a saved hub removes its stored access token. Clearing activity removes local activity entries. Erasing local data removes the workspace, saved credentials and explicit language preference. Exported files, hub records and third-party records must be managed separately.

You can follow the data-deletion instructions without creating an account. Contact Sakhi Apps through our GitHub support form for help or an applicable privacy-rights request. Do not send hub tokens, passwords or sensitive home data. We can help you locate local controls but cannot remotely erase information held only on your device or by your hub operator.

Support and this website

If you contact support, your contact identifier and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. GitHub support requests are public: use a general description and never include personal or sensitive home information. Share only what is needed. You can request deletion of support correspondence through the same contact, subject to any applicable record-keeping obligations.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; its privacy statement governs that processing. We have added no website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact forms.

Security and changes

The app requires HTTPS for hub connections and uses encrypted Android storage for saved credentials. No device, host or security measure is risk-free. Keep your device and hub updated and revoke a hub token if you believe it has been exposed.

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date. Material service changes will also be described with the applicable app update. Privacy and support contact: Sakhi Apps through our GitHub support form.
